# Dragon Realm World Book

---

# **Purpose**

Dragon Realm is the first official world of Nimpol Chronicles.

It was created by William and serves as the flagship world for the platform.

Dragon Realm is designed to encourage exploration, curiosity, creativity, courage, and growth.

The AI should treat Dragon Realm as a real place with consistent geography, history, magic, and inhabitants.

While every child's Chronicle is unique, Dragon Realm itself remains internally consistent.

Children may discover new places, create new characters, and expand the world over time, but existing locations should respect the world's established rules.

---



# **World Philosophy**

Dragon Realm is not a world built around combat.

It is a world built around discovery.

Challenges may involve:

- exploration  
- mystery  
- helping others  
- solving problems  
- magical puzzles  
- diplomacy  
- courage  
- kindness  
- creativity

Combat exists.

But it is only one way to solve problems.

# **Geography**

---



## **🌳 The Hidden Treehouse**



### Role:

- Home Base



### Purpose:

- Safe location.  
- Resting.  
- Inventory.  
- Talking with Mom.  
- Receiving letters.  
- Planning adventures.  
- Seasonal decorations.  
- Pet interactions.  
- The treehouse should gradually evolve as the child grows.

---



## **🌳 Tree of Life and Death**



### Purpose

- Represents balance.  
- Life.  
- Death.  
- Renewal.  
- Growth.  
- Nearby Flora  
  - Blue Plants  
  - Help red plants grow.  
  - Red Plants  
  - Depend on blue plants.
- The glowing sap has magical properties.  
- There is a symbiotic relationship.  
- The tree should remain mysterious throughout the game.  
- Not every mystery should be solved.

---



## **🏘️ Village**



### Purpose

- Friendly NPCs.  
- Merchants.  
- Craftsmen.  
- Festivals.  
- News.  
- Rumors.  
- Side quests.  
- Children.  
- Animals.  
- The village should feel alive.  
- NPCs remember previous encounters.

---



## **🌊 Great Serpent River**



### Purpose

- Natural barrier.  
- Sea serpents prevent ordinary crossing.  
- Possible solutions  
  - Dragon assistance  
  - Boat  
  - Magic  
  - Bridge
- Friendship  
- Later abilities  
- The river becomes a progression gate.

---



## **🐉 Dragon Caves**



### Purpose

- Home of the dragons.  
- Dragons are respected.  
- Mostly kind.  
- Intelligent.  
- Protectors of balance.  
- Different dragons have different personalities.  
  - Children.  
  - Elders.  
  - Historians.  
  - Inventors.  
  - Explorers.
- No two dragons should feel identical.

---



## **❄ Mount Icy Tip**



### Contains

- Large water cave.  
- Ice magic.  
- Rare crystals.  
- Cold creatures.  
- Hidden passages.

---



## **🌄 Mount Spikey Trap**

- Covered in tiny magical thorns.  
- Dragons dislike landing here.  
- Difficult climbing.  
- Natural hazards.

---



## **☀ Mount Tippy Top**

- The source of the world's magic.  
- A glowing yellow sphere produces magical energy.  
- Gravity becomes lighter near the summit.  
- Only experienced adventurers should reach the top.  
- The mystery of the glowing sphere should never be fully explained.

---



## **⛰️ Disappearos Mountain**

- A magical mountain under an ancient spell.  
- Sometimes disappears.  
- Leaves behind an enormous crater the same shape as the mountain.  
- Its appearances become world events.  
- Every return may reveal something different.

---



## **🏛 Temple**

- Surrounded by magical water.  
- Protected by an ancient magical barrier.  
- Requires significant magical ability to enter.  
- Barrier opens temporarily.  
- Inside are ancient magical secrets.  
- The temple should remain relevant throughout the entire game.

---



# **Magic**

---

Magic is part of everyday life.

Simple magic is common.

Complex magic requires training.

Great magic requires wisdom as much as power.

Magic is strongest near Mount Tippy Top.

Magic is not unlimited.

Power should always have purpose.

---

# **Nutty (Side-kick)**

I think Nutty deserves his own section.

Not because he's a companion.

Because he's practically a second protagonist.

### He should:

- Find hidden things.  
- Cause occasional trouble.  
- Notice secrets.  
- Distract enemies.  
- Encourage exploration.  
- React emotionally.  
- Become braver over time.  
- Learn.  
- Remember.

---



# **Mom**

Mom is not simply an NPC.

She represents:

- Safety.  
- Healing.  
- Encouragement.  
- Guidance.  
- Home.  
- The AI should never portray Mom as controlling.  
- She supports.  
- She never commands.

---



# **Story Rules**



### The AI MAY

- Create new side characters.  
- Create small discoveries.  
- Create temporary mysteries.  
- Create weather.  
- Create celebrations.  
- Create rumors.  
- Create small adventures.  
- Ask the child to invent.

---



### The AI MUST NOT

- Move existing locations.  
- Change world geography.  
- Rewrite established history.  
- Change important characters without permission.  
- Invent educational resources.  
- Solve every mystery.  
- Take creative control away from the child.



# **Appendix A — World Constants**

- Dragon Realm is a world where magic is real.  
- Mount Tippy Top is the primary source of magical energy.  
- Dragons are generally respected and are protectors of balance.  
- Nutty is always Nimpol's loyal companion unless the story explicitly separates them temporarily.  
- Mom always represents safety, healing, and encouragement.  
- The Hidden Treehouse is always a safe place.  
- The Tree of Life and Death always represents balance.  
- Dragon Realm values curiosity over violence.  
- Combat is never the only solution.



# **Appendix B — World Themes**



### Dragon Realm should consistently evoke:

- Wonder  
- Discovery  
- Hope  
- Courage  
- Kindness

- Curiosity
